 📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard for Mobile Sales Performance Analysis



 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI

The objective is to transform raw mobile sales transactions into meaningful business insights that help stakeholders understand sales performance, product performance, geographic trends, customer behaviour, payment preferences, and sales patterns over time.

The dashboard follows a complete data analytics workflow:

Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Data Modelling → DAX → Visualization → Business Insights

 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- Which brands and mobile models generate the highest sales?
- Which cities are the strongest sales markets?
- How do sales change across months and days?
- Which payment methods are most frequently used?
- How are customer ratings distributed?
- Which products and markets may require further business attention?
- How can sales data support inventory, marketing, and sales planning?

 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

1. 🗺️ Total Sales by City — Map

 Why this visual?

A Map visual was selected to analyse the geographic distribution of sales.

It makes it easier to identify:

- High-performing cities
- Low-performing markets
- Regional sales concentration
- Geographic sales patterns

 Business Value

This analysis can support regional sales planning, inventory allocation, distribution decisions, and location-specific marketing strategies.

2. 📈 Monthly Sales Trend — Line Chart

Why this visual?

A Line Chart is appropriate for analysing changes over time.

The visualization shows monthly sales/quantity trends and helps identify:

- Increasing or decreasing sales
- High-performing months
- Low-performing months
- Potential seasonal patterns

 Business Value

Monthly trend analysis can support inventory planning, promotional campaigns, sales planning, and performance monitoring. 

 
 3. 🥧 Transactions by Payment Method — Pie Chart

 Why this visual?

A Pie Chart was used to show the contribution of different payment methods to total transactions.

The dashboard analyses payment methods such as:

- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- Cash

   Business Value

Understanding payment preferences can help businesses improve their checkout experience, digital payment strategy, and customer convenience.


 4. 📊 Total Sales by Mobile Model — Bar Chart

 Why this visual?

A Bar Chart is effective for comparing sales performance across multiple mobile models.

The chart helps identify:

- Top-performing models

5. 🔻 Customer Ratings — Funnel Chart

 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters

- 📅 Day Name

These filters allow users to perform dynamic, focused analysis without creating separate reports.

Example

A user can select:

Brand → Apple
Payment Method → UPI
Month → July

and analyse the corresponding sales performance across the dashboard.

🔍 Key Business Insights

 🏆 Brand Performance

Apple generated approximately ₹161.62M in sales, followed by Samsung at approximately ₹160.04M.

The relatively balanced contribution among the major brands indicates that sales are distributed across multiple brands.

 🌍 Geographic Performance

Delhi was the highest-sales city, generating approximately ₹203.9M in total sales.

This highlights Delhi as a significant market within the dataset.

📱 Product Performance

iPhone SE was the top-performing mobile model by total sales, generating approximately ₹59.6M.

This type of analysis can help identify products requiring greater inventory attention or promotional focus.

 💳 Payment Behaviour

UPI represented approximately 26.36% of transactions, making it the most frequently used payment method in the dataset.

⭐ Customer Experience

The average customer rating was approximately 3.69/5, providing a baseline metric for monitoring customer satisfaction.
